# Italian Game

The Italian Game is the opening 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4. It is a direct application of the [[Opening principles]]: White takes the centre with a pawn, develops a knight towards it with tempo on e5, then develops the bishop to an active diagonal before castling.

From c4 the bishop aims at f7, the square in Black's camp that only the king defends, which makes it an early target. If Black answers with 3...Nf6, the [[Fried Liver Attack]] is one way White goes after it, bringing a knight to g5 to attack f7 a second time.
