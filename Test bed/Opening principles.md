# Opening principles

In the opening, each move should help with one of three things: bring a minor piece off the back rank, put a pawn or piece on or against the four centre squares, or get the king castled. A move that does none of these, such as moving the same piece twice or pushing a flank pawn, usually hands the tempo to the opponent.
