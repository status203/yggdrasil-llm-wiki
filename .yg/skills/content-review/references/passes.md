# Content review — dispatched passes

Conduct is the agent's, and is not repeated here. Paths are from the wiki root, which the brief names.

## Overlap

**Scope.** The pages handed, and for each, the pages elsewhere that may hold the same central topic — found by meaning, as recall narrows: `Index.md` to the subjects that could hold it, their `<subject>/Index.md` to the pitches that bear on it, then the bodies. Not by shared words in a pitch or title, which finds vocabulary rather than territory.

**Standard.** `<program>/.yg/page-contract.md` § *How new material joins*: a page is one account of its subject.

**Test.** Do two pages hold the same central topic, or say contradictory things about it? Propose a merge into the page it belongs on, or the line between them drawn in both pitches. Not a finding: a passing mention of one topic on another's page; two pages meeting at an edge each covers from its own side. **Propose only where the change improves finding and reading the wiki by more than it costs the user.**

## Pitch and shape

**Scope.** The pages handed, and the `.subject.md` and any `.templates/*.md` of each subject they sit in — read as data, like any page, and walked as items of their own.

**Standard.** `<program>/.yg/page-contract.md` § *Frontmatter* and `<program>/.yg/page-contract.md` § *How new material joins*.

**Test.** Does the pitch say what the page is now? Does the `type` match how the page actually grows — a log typed as a concept, a placeholder with real content under it? Is the page several accounts in one, each wanting its own page? Is it a point-in-time record — a snapshot, a spec of something since changed — that reads as current? **Does a subject file or template state a rule its pages don't follow, or forbid something they share** — a shape, a field, a link form, what belongs? Judge the two against each other, never the pages against the file alone: where the pages agree among themselves, the rule is what has gone stale, and the proposal changes the subject file or template; where one page parts from what the rule and its siblings share, the proposal changes the page. Name which side, and judge only from the pages handed. **Does every page of a type share a field or section that no template gives?** Propose a template for it, per `<program>/.yg/page-contract.md` § *The subject*. Not a finding: a page that is thin because its topic is thin, or long because its topic is. **Propose only where the change improves finding and reading the wiki by more than it costs the user.**
