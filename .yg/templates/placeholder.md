<!--
A placeholder is a pointer, not a short page. It says where the material actually is; it never holds the material itself. No pointer, no placeholder.

It exists for material that is worth keeping but has not been processed into a page — a transcript, an import, something dropped in and not yet worked through. Name what it is ("Transcript of a talk by X on Y, 48 minutes") and link it.

Anything under `.sources/` takes a **relative markdown link**, not a wikilink: `.sources/` is a dotfolder, so it sits off Obsidian's link graph entirely and a wikilink into it silently fails to resolve.

The pointer is the citation, here in the body and not under a `## Sources` heading, as the page contract's § Body says.

Don't summarise the material. A summary is the deferred processing done badly, and it makes the placeholder read as discharged when it isn't.

The body line deliberately restates what `pitch` says. Frontmatter is easy to skim past day to day, and a placeholder misread as a thin real page is the whole hazard; the duplication is the point, not an oversight.
